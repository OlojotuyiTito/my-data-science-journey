# Day 1 - DS Journey

Today I went on to really understand what Data Science is all about so as to know what I'm actually getting myself into.

These are my takeaways from the IBM Course - What is Data Science (Coursera)

At the very core of it and in its essence, data science is simply the process of working with data to uncover insights and trends, using them to answer
questions and make informed decisions.

Sounds simple right? Yeah, it is!

You're not a Data Scientist by the tools you use but by your ability to translate raw facts into informed data-driven decisions.

Look at this for example:

A basketball team didn't perform well last season are reminiscing on what could've gone wrong.

Their questions is simple: How could we have scored more goals last season?

Normally, the answer would just be assumed. Maybe they would say "We didn't train hard enough".

But that's a vague answer and doesn't particularly proffer a solution.

A data scientist would ask "What data can I use to answer this question"

He them gets the recordings of all the team's games of last season.

After analyzing that data, he found out that majority of the team's successful baskets were three-pointers.

There's the answer right there. The team is better at scoring three-pointers than other kinds of baskets.

So if they want to score more, they should focus on getting more three-pointers.

That's a practical application of how Data Science solves problems.

And in bigger companies and industries, data science can help reduce costs, increase revenue, avoid customer churn,
discover better advertising techniques and much more.

This is __DATA SCIENCE__.
